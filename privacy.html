Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Application: PixelCode QR (Web & Android App: com.pixelcode.qr)

1. Overview & Core Commitment

At PixelCode (pixelcode.in), your privacy is our absolute priority. PixelCode QR is designed from the ground up to be a 100% on-device, private utility.

Unlike online QR generators that route your visitors through expiring middleman tracking servers, PixelCode QR builds permanent, static vector QR codes directly in your device's memory. We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or sell any information you input into the app.

2. Data Collection & Processing

PixelCode QR collects ZERO personal data:

  • No Account Required: You never need to sign up, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the app.
  • No Scan Tracking: We do not log where, when, or how many times your generated QR codes are scanned.
  • No Telemetry or User Profiling: We do not deploy third-party advertising SDKs, behavioural trackers, or analytics cookies.
  • Local Matrix Computation: All QR generation (URLs, UPI payment text strings, Wi-Fi passwords, contact vCards) is computed locally on your device via client-side JavaScript / HTML5 canvas.

3. Device Permissions Policy

Our Android application (com.pixelcode.qr) operates strictly under the Principle of Least Privilege:

  • No Camera Access: The app does not request or require camera permissions.
  • No Location Access: The app does not request or track your GPS or network location.
  • No Contacts Access: Creating a vCard contact QR is done solely by manually typing the details into the form. We never read your phone's address book.
  • No Sensitive Storage Permissions: Images, SVGs, and PDF flyers are exported via Android's secure FileProvider and standard download triggers without requesting invasive broad storage access.
  • Internet Permission: Used strictly for loading web styling resources and optional outbound links you click (such as WhatsApp support). All cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled.

4. Financial & Payment Features Disclaimer

PixelCode QR provides design templates for UPI QR payment standees (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM):

  • PixelCode QR is NOT a financial institution, banking app, payment gateway, or money transmitter.
  • The app simply converts standard payment string formats (e.g. upi://pay?pa=...) into a visual QR code matrix for physical desktop/counter acrylic standees.
  • We never access, store, or process your bank account details, credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or financial transactions.

5. Network Security & Encryption

Our mobile application enforces Android's strict Network Security Configuration:

  • Unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic is strictly blocked (cleartextTrafficPermitted="false").
  • All communications to external web properties are encrypted via industry-standard TLS / HTTPS.
  • Mixed content is fully disabled in the embedded WebView.

6. Children's Privacy

PixelCode QR is a general utility and productivity tool intended for businesses, creators, and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe any child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technological or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated Effective Date.
