Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about generating, printing, and deploying commercial QR codes.

Are QR codes generated on PixelCode really 100% free and permanent? ▼

Yes, absolutely! All static QR codes generated by PixelCode QR are 100% free forever and never expire. Unlike competitor platforms that shut off your codes after 14 days to force a paid subscription, our engine encodes your destination link directly into the QR matrix. There are no middleman servers, no redirects, and no expiration dates—your codes work permanently for life.

Can I add my company logo and brand colors to the QR code? ▼

Yes! You can upload any PNG, JPG, or SVG company logo. Our engine automatically centers the logo and activates high Error Correction Level H (30% error tolerance), ensuring that your code remains 100% instantly scannable by all modern smartphone cameras without data loss.

Can I download vector SVG and print-ready PDF files for physical printing? ▼

Yes. PixelCode QR gives you direct, one-click access to infinite-resolution Vector SVG and commercial print-ready PDF files with embedded typography and standee graphics. These vector files never pixelate or blur, making them ideal for acrylic desk standees, counter tents, banners, and billboards.

How do I generate a UPI payment QR code standee for Google Pay and PhonePe? ▼

Simply click the "UPI Pay" tab in Step 1, enter your Virtual Payment Address (e.g. yourname@okaxis or business@upi) along with your business name, and select the "UPI / Pay Standee" template. Your code is instantly formatted with official UPI payment cues supporting GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.

Do I need to sign up or provide an email to download my QR code? ▼

No registration, credit card, or email address is ever required. PixelCode QR operates directly in your browser with complete client-side processing, respecting your time and data privacy.

What is the minimum recommended print size for commercial QR codes? ▼

For standard business cards, menus, and table standees, a minimum size of 2 × 2 cm (0.8 × 0.8 inches) is recommended. For larger standees and counter cards, 5 × 5 cm (2 × 2 inches) provides effortless scanning from up to 1.5 meters away. Always preserve the quiet zone (white padding around the code) for reliable recognition.
